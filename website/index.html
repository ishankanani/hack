How it works

From photo to understanding in six steps.

Everything that happens between sending your question and getting the answer. Choose a step to see it in the chat.

  1. 01

    Take a photo of the sum from your textbook, worksheet or notebook. Or type it, like 3x − 7 = 11.

    Printed or handwritten, both work
  2. 02

    A vision model turns the photo into maths and shows you exactly what it read. If something is off, tap Fix and type it. That retry is free.

    Your photo is deleted after reading
  3. 03

    An AI maths teacher works through the problem in short steps, pitched at your class and explained in English or Hinglish.

    Simple equations are answered by our maths engine directly
  4. 04

    A separate maths engine checks the result before you see it. It puts roots back into the equation, differentiates integrals and recomputes numbers. If the check fails, a second AI solves it again from scratch.

    Passed answers get the Verified badge
  5. 05

    You get the answer, the key idea, numbered steps and the mistake students usually make. Stuck on a step? Ask for a hint, the next step, or why.

    Tutor mode gives hints first, the answer when you ask
  6. 06

    Tap for a handwritten solution page with your steps, the verified answer and a QR code. Anyone who scans it opens the same solution.

    Questions solved before come back instantly, free

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Write to support@mathfix.ai.

Is MathFix really free?

Yes. Every student gets 5 solves and 15 tutor messages a day at no cost, and there are no ads. If a photo can't be read or we're busy, it doesn't count.

Do I need to install an app?

Only Telegram. Open @mathfixbot, press Start and answer two quick questions. Android and iPhone apps are coming later.

How accurate are the answers?

Wherever the problem allows it, a maths engine checks the answer before you see it, and passed answers are marked Verified. Some problems, like proofs, can't be checked by a machine; those are labelled so you know to review the steps. If you spot a mistake, tap Wrong and we'll look at it.

What happens to my photos and data?

We read the photo and then delete it. We keep your Telegram first name, your class and language choice, and how many solves you used. Tutor chats are deleted after 7 days. Send /deleteme to remove your data.

Can parents and teachers use it?

Yes. Choose Parent / Teacher during setup and the explanations focus on the method, so you can teach it the same way.

Can I use it in Hinglish?

Yes. Choose Hinglish during setup, or any time with /lang. The steps are explained in simple Hindi written in English letters, while the maths stays in standard notation.
